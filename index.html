How these were checked, and what's still unknown

Two things can go wrong, and both did. A career page that renders a full job description is not proof the req is open — several companies serve complete cached pages for closed roles. But absence from a listing page is not proof it is closed either: that test wrongly killed four live Datadog and Google roles before a per-req check brought them back. The standard here is a single one — a per-req lookup against the company's own applicant-tracking feed, which returns a hard 404 when a req is gone. Five roles have now failed that. Where no such endpoint exists, the row is kept and flagged rather than guessed at.

Two routes, and the board now filters between them. Every row is tagged by whether the requisition actually wants you to manage people. 59 require no reports; 38 ask you to run a PM team. The IC half is not a consolation prize: 39 of those 59 carry a band topping $240K, and at platform companies the Staff and Principal bands are frequently identical to the Group PM band — Datadog pays $244–305K for both. So the people-leadership gap on the résumé gates roughly 38 rows, not the board. The honest cost of the IC route is the title, not the money: stepping Director → Staff is easy and stepping back is not. Use the Reports filter to work one route at a time.

Anthropic is now verifiable. Their careers page server-renders the complete list of all 645 open requisitions, and it is reachable from here — so Anthropic open/closed is authoritative for the first time rather than inferred from search. Checking the twelve Anthropic rows this board carried: nine were dead. Among them both roles previously flagged here as the level-appropriate alternatives to Labs — Head of Product, Claude Developer Platform and Lead Product Manager, Developer Services. Only Human Data Platform, Research (Code) and Claude Science survive, and nine newly-opened Anthropic product reqs have been added. A harder structural finding: across all 645 open reqs Anthropic has no open product-leadership role of any kind — no Head of Product, Director, Group PM, VP, Principal or Staff PM, and not even a Senior PM title; every product opening is a flat “Product Manager, X”. That is verified fact, not a search gap, and it means there is currently no Anthropic target at Director level at all.

7 Oct sweep — four more Datadog deaths in fourteen days. Datadog is still the only careers domain this environment can reach, and re-checking it against its own sitemap killed four rows: Senior PM Data Agent (which was a fit 10), Staff PM Observability Data Platforms, Senior PM Search (the explicit MCP match) and Senior PM Observability Data Platform. A fifth req was silently retitled from “Bits Release” to “Self Improving Software.” That is four closures and one rewrite in two weeks at the one employer where the truth is checkable — which is the best available estimate of how fast the sixty-plus unverifiable rows are rotting too. Treat every row without a 7 Oct date as a lead. Anthropic Labs: rejected 25 Sep, kept on the board as a record.

Built In, Lensa, The Muse and Glassdoor were all still serving it, which is the cached-page failure in its purest form: every one of those links dead-ends at the same 404.
