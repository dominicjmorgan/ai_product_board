How these were checked, and what's still unknown

Two things can go wrong, and both did. A career page that renders a full job description is not proof the req is open — several companies serve complete cached pages for closed roles. But absence from a listing page is not proof it is closed either: that test wrongly killed four live Datadog and Google roles before a per-req check brought them back. The standard here is now a single one — a per-req lookup against the company's own applicant-tracking feed, which returns a hard 404 when a req is gone. Four roles failed that. Where no such endpoint exists, the row is kept and flagged rather than guessed at.

Kept but unconfirmable

  • Google, Intuit, Etsy, Stripe publish no per-req endpoint. Rows carry a warning; the postings are live and indexed, but a small chance of closure remains.
  • Adobe Bridgeway — blocks automated reads entirely; New York may or may not be a valid location. Ask a recruiter before writing anything bespoke.
  • Anthropic Claude Tag — its own feed contradicts its own posting on whether NYC counts.

Blocked — check by hand

  • Meta and Microsoft Corp / LinkedIn / GitHub — client-rendered or robots-blocked. All three certainly have NYC PM reqs.
  • Salesforce Agentforce and ServiceNow AI Platform — the two best conceptual fits in the whole target list, neither verifiable here.
  • Goldman Sachs, NVIDIA, Warner Bros. Discovery — portals blocked.

Real negatives

  • OpenAI — 11 PM reqs open, every one San Francisco.
  • Apple — no NYC PM reqs at all; NYC headcount is ML engineering and retail.
  • Snowflake — product org sits in the Bay Area and Bellevue.
  • Spotify, Squarespace, xAI, Perplexity, Glean — nothing NYC in product.

The level problem

  • Every Amazon NYC agent role sits at Senior PMT, one rung below you — the AWS Developer Tools Director is the only same-level move inside the company.
  • Google tops out at Group PM for Director-equivalent scope, and its one remaining GPM opening is among its weakest content fits.
  • Anthropic and Netflix pay a Senior PM more than most Director bands here, so title and comp point in opposite directions. Anthropic Enterprise alone is $385–595K.
  • Bloomberg is now the densest same-level target: two Lead roles at $180–350K, both AI, both New York.
